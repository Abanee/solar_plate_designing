05 / ENGINEERING DISCIPLINE

Every Installation
Starts With The Details.

A solar installation is an electrical system integrated into your home. Here is how our engineering flows from roof to grid.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE / FIT THE SYSTEM TO THE PROPERTY
01

Roof Assessment & Orientation

Analyzing tilt angle, load-bearing capacity, and shade obstacles before mounting design begins.

02

Tier-1 Photovoltaic Modules

Selecting anti-reflective, high-efficiency monocrystalline solar panels built for high heat resistance.

03

Weather-Proof Mounting

Corrosion-resistant aluminum racking secured directly to roof structure with dual-seal waterproofing.

04

Concealed DC Wiring

UV-rated solar cables enclosed in rigid conduits protecting against weather and electrical degradation.

05

Synchronized Smart Inverter

High-efficiency inversion converting DC energy to synchronized household AC power with surge protection.

06

Bi-Directional Grid Integration

Safely connected to main distribution panel with net-metering synchronization for excess power return.
