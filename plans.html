02 / LABOR & MOUNTING FEES

Fixing Charges
Itemized Cost Schedule.

Know exactly where your money goes. Below is our standard fee schedule for rooftop fixing, structural mounting, electrical labor, and grid connection.

Rooftop Racking & Structural Fixing

Drilling, chemical anchoring, anodized aluminum rail assembly, panel clamp tightening, and roof waterproofing sealant.

₹4,000 / kW

Electrical Conduit & Cable Wiring

UV-resistant DC solar cable routing, AC main panel conduit wiring, MC4 crimping, and wall trunking installation.

₹2,500 / kW

Chemical Earthing & Surge Protection

Excavation & installation of copper-bonded chemical earthing pits, lightning arrester fixing, and AC/DC SPD wiring.

₹4,500 Flat

DISCOM Net Metering & Grid Inspection

Utility DISCOM application submission, electrical inspector coordination, bi-directional net meter fixing, and grid synchronization.

₹5,000 Flat
NOTE ON FIXING CHARGES: All fixing charges are 100% transparent and included in our final turnkey plan prices above. No extra charges are added unless special elevated superstructures (e.g. 10ft high gazebo frames) are requested.
